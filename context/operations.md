# Kicksta — Operations & Tools

## My Primary Tools

### Customer.io
- **What it's used for:** ALL email marketing
  - Lifecycle email automations (onboarding, activation, retention, churn)
  - Broadcast campaigns (newsletters, announcements, promos)
  - Transactional emails (receipts, plan changes, account notices)
  - Behavioral triggers (in-app events → email flows)
- **Key concepts:** Segments, Campaigns, Broadcasts, Workflows, Attributes, Events
- **Integration:** Likely synced with Chargebee for subscription events

### Chargebee
- **What it's used for:** ALL customer and subscription data
  - Subscription management (create, pause, cancel, upgrade, downgrade)
  - Billing and invoicing
  - MRR tracking and revenue reporting
  - Churn data and cancellation reasons
  - Plan and coupon management
- **Key data available:** Plan type (Growth/Advanced), billing cycle (monthly/annual),
  MRR contribution, subscription status, trial status, cancellation date/reason

## Customer Lifecycle (Kicksta)

1. **Trial start** — 7-day free trial begins
2. **Activation** — user connects Instagram account, growth starts
3. **Trial conversion** — user upgrades to paid (Growth or Advanced, monthly or annual)
4. **Active subscriber** — receiving follower growth, using dashboard
5. **Upgrade opportunity** — Growth → Advanced, monthly → annual
6. **At-risk / churning** — usage drops, support tickets, plan pause
7. **Cancellation** — subscription ends
8. **Win-back** — lapsed customer re-engagement
