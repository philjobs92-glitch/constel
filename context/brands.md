---

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Do
- Lead with outcomes: followers, engagement, reach, conversions
- Use specific numbers whenever possible (e.g., "800–1,200 new followers/month")
- Reference the 10-year track record and 100k+ customers for credibility
- Emphasize "organic" and "real" — these are core differentiators vs. bots/fake services
- Use social proof (reviews, G2 awards, engagement stats)
- Keep sentences short and punchy in email subject lines and ads

### Don't
- Say "take your Instagram to the next level" or other generic growth clichés
- Over-promise or use vague superlatives ("the best ever", "totally amazing")
- Use jargon the average creator or small business owner wouldn't understand
- Sound like a bot wrote it
